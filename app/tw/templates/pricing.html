Questions

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Sign up with your phone number and you get {{ trial_days }} days of everything. We only ask for payment when you choose to subscribe.

How can I pay?

In Ethiopia: Telebirr, CBE Birr, a bank account or a card on Chapa's secure page, or a Telebirr or bank transfer to Sonador Systems that we record for you. From abroad: an international card in US dollars on Chapa, or a USD or EUR bank transfer.

Can my firm pay in US dollars?

Yes. Pick US dollars above. Watch is {{ "USD {:,}".format(all_prices.USD.month) }} a month or {{ "USD {:,}".format(all_prices.USD.year) }} a year; Win is {{ "USD {:,}".format(all_prices.USD.win_month) }} a month or {{ "USD {:,}".format(all_prices.USD.win_year) }} a year.

What does Bid Room do?

Press "Start this bid" on any tender. Bid Room reads the notice, and the bidding document if you paste it in, and lists every requirement as a checklist marked met, gap or check against your company profile. It drafts the cover letter, company section and technical offer from your profile, you add your prices, and it prints a bid with your letterhead, ready to sign and stamp. Anything it can't know is marked [TO CONFIRM] for you to fill in. It is in the Win plan and in the free trial.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes. Ask us and Sonador Systems sends an invoice for your payment, in your company's name and with your TIN.

What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

You can still browse open tenders. The full notices, your matches and the morning alerts stop until you pay. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews on its own.

Where do the tenders come from?

Our collectors photograph notices in the newspapers and on buyers' boards, and our software reads government, bank, NGO and donor websites. A person checks every tender before it goes up.

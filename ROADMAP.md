# Roadmap to launch: 31 Dec 2026

Today is 26 Oct, so there are about 9 weeks. Rule: a paying or committed customer beats a finished feature.

| Week | Dates | Goal | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 26 Oct-1 Nov | Pick ONE sector. List 5 candidate sources and check terms. Build first scraper + storage | 50+ real tenders in the database |
| 2 | 2-8 Nov | LLM extraction + matching. Digest sent to yourself | Deadlines correct on 20 of 20 hand-checked tenders |
| 3 | 9-15 Nov | Free digest to 10 companies (WhatsApp/Telegram, by hand if needed) | 10 companies receiving it; 5 calls done |
| 4 | 16-22 Nov | Fix what they said. Add second source. Amharic handling | 3+ say they'd miss it |
| 5 | 23-29 Nov | Telegram bot delivery + company profile onboarding | Self-serve signup works |
| 6 | 30 Nov-6 Dec | Pricing test. Bid-draft prototype for paid tier | 3 companies quoted a price |
| 7 | 7-13 Dec | Payments (manual/Telebirr first). Landing page | First payment or written commitment |
| 8 | 14-20 Dec | Reliability: monitoring, alerts when a source breaks. Onboard first 5 paying | 5 on the paid plan or in trial |
| 9 | 21-31 Dec | Public launch: post, outreach, referrals | Launch live by 31 Dec |

## Go/no-go checkpoints
- **15 Nov:** if fewer than 3 of 10 companies engage, change sector or buyer type before building more.
- **6 Dec:** if no one will name a price, fix the offer, not the code.

## Open questions
- Which sector first (construction, IT supply, medical, consulting)?
- Which sources are legally and technically scrapable? (verify, don't assume)
- Pricing: monthly per company, or per sector?
